# v2.7.1 - Critical Backtest Bug Fix

## What Was Fixed

**Problem:** All strategies showed identical accuracy and winrate in backtests, making it impossible to compare strategy performance.

**Root Cause:** The backtest was using a **different indicator suite** than the live signal path:
- **Live signals** (content.js): Used `lean: false` → FULL indicator suite (15 indicators)
- **Backtest** (backtest.js): Defaulted to `lean: true` → LEAN indicator suite (11 indicators)

**Missing Indicators in Backtest:**
1. **Donchian Channels** - Critical for breakout strategies (weight 3-4)
2. **Williams %R** - Used by scalp/mean-reversion strategies
3. **CCI** - Momentum confirmation for scalp/confluence
4. **Hurst Exponent** - Trend persistence for institutional_flow (weight 3)

Without these indicators, strategies that differ primarily in how they use these signals appeared identical.

## Technical Details

### Why This Matters

The 4 missing indicators are **strategy differentiators**:

**Donchian Channels** (breakout detection):
- `breakout` strategy: weight 4
- `turbo_trend`: weight 3
- `sniper`: weight 3
- Without this, breakout strategies can't detect price breakouts

**Hurst Exponent** (trend persistence):
- `institutional_flow`: weight 3
- `squeeze`: weight 2
- `breakout`: weight 2
- Without this, institutional flow can't detect persistent trends

**Williams %R** (mean-reversion):
- `scalp`: weight 1
- `confluence`: weight 1
- Without this, scalp strategies lose mean-reversion signals

**CCI** (momentum):
- `scalp`: weight 1
- `confluence`: weight 1
- Without this, momentum confirmation is missing

### Performance Trade-off

**Slower backtests:** The full `analyze()` function is called for each bar instead of the optimized `evaluateLeanAt()`.

**Typical impact:**
- 7 days, 12 strategies, 20 assets: ~20-30 seconds (was ~5-10 seconds)
- Still acceptable for accuracy and correctness

**Why it's worth it:**
- Backtest now matches live trading exactly
- Strategies show real performance differences
- Users can make informed strategy choices
